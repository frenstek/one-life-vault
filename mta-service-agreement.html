2 Purpose and Goals

This agreement sets out the terms under which One Life Foundation will deliver Supported Independent Living (SIL) supports to the participant during their Medium Term Accommodation (MTA) placement (up to 90 days). MTA is transitional accommodation funded under the participant's NDIS plan while a longer-term housing solution is identified.

One Life Foundation is the SIL provider — responsible for delivering daily support services within the MTA property. The property itself is managed by a separate SDA/MTA provider under a separate agreement.

This agreement covers the MTA period (up to 90 days). If an extension beyond 90 days is required, it must be approved by the NDIA through a plan review. One Life Foundation will support the participant to request an extension if needed. The agreement may also end earlier if the participant transitions to longer-term accommodation.

4 Schedule of Supports

A detailed Schedule of Supports is maintained as a separate document (Attachment A) and forms part of this agreement. It includes specific support items, line item numbers, frequencies, and delivery details. The schedule below provides a summary of the types of supports delivered during the MTA placement.

Supports Included

  • Personal care (showering, toileting, grooming, dressing, oral hygiene).
  • Daily living tasks (meal preparation, cleaning, laundry, household tasks).
  • Overnight support (active night or sleepover as per assessed needs).
  • Medication prompting and/or administration as per medication management plan.
  • Skill building and capacity development to increase independence.
  • Health and wellbeing support (appointments, exercise, health monitoring).
  • Community access, social participation, and relationship building.
  • Communication and decision-making support.
  • Behaviour support plan implementation (if applicable).
  • Emergency response and on-call support.
  • Coordination with the MTA/SDA property provider and other service providers.
  • Transition planning support towards longer-term accommodation.

Supports Not Included

  • Accommodation, property management, or tenancy matters (managed by the MTA/SDA property provider).
  • Property maintenance, repairs, or utilities (property provider / participant responsibility).
  • Transport not directly related to support delivery (funded separately).
  • Allied health services (funded separately).
  • Support coordination (funded separately).

Workers with the same qualifications and experience will be assigned as a delegate to ensure the same level of support is provided and adequate training will be provided to them.

6 Provider Obligations

  • Deliver supports in a person-centred, safe, and competent manner.
  • Comply with the NDIS Act 2013 (Cth), NDIS Practice Standards, and NDIS Code of Conduct.
  • Maintain current NDIS registration for the duration of this agreement.
  • Ensure all workers hold a current NDIS Worker Screening Check clearance.
  • Ensure workers are trained in disability support, manual handling, first aid, medication management, and any participant-specific requirements.
  • Introduce new support workers to the participant before unsupervised shifts where practicable.
  • Maintain Public Liability Insurance (min. $20M), Professional Indemnity Insurance, and Workers Compensation Insurance. Certificates available on request.
  • Cooperate and coordinate with the MTA/SDA property provider to ensure seamless service delivery.
  • Comply with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and Australian Privacy Principles.
  • Develop and maintain an individual support plan, reviewed at least every 3 months.

7 NDIS Code of Conduct

One Life Foundation and all workers are bound by the NDIS Code of Conduct (National Disability Insurance Scheme (Code of Conduct) Rules 2018). We will:

  1. Act with respect for individual rights to freedom of expression, self-determination, and decision-making.
  2. Respect the privacy of people with disability.
  3. Provide supports in a safe and competent manner, with care and skill.
  4. Act with integrity, honesty, and transparency.
  5. Promptly raise and act on concerns about quality and safety of supports.
  6. Take all reasonable steps to prevent and respond to violence, exploitation, neglect, and abuse.
  7. Take all reasonable steps to prevent and respond to sexual misconduct.

Code of Conduct concerns can be raised with One Life Foundation or the NDIS Commission on 1800 035 544.

8 Participant Rights

As set out in the NDIS Act 2013 and the UN Convention on the Rights of Persons with Disabilities:

  • Be treated with dignity, respect, and fairness at all times.
  • Exercise informed choice and control over your supports.
  • Dignity of risk — make your own choices even when others may disagree.
  • Privacy and confidentiality of your personal information.
  • Services free from violence, abuse, neglect, exploitation, and discrimination.
  • Access an advocate or support person at any time.
  • Choose and change your support provider at any time without penalty.
  • Have your cultural identity, values, and beliefs respected.
  • Be supported to understand this agreement in your preferred format.
  • Give feedback or complain without fear of reprisal.
  • Be involved in decisions about your roster, staffing, and supports.

9 Participant Responsibilities

  • Treat support workers and other residents with respect.
  • Provide accurate information relevant to the delivery of supports.
  • Notify One Life Foundation of changes to your NDIS plan or personal circumstances.
  • Give reasonable notice of absences (holidays, hospital stays) for roster adjustments.
  • Participate in the development and review of your support plan and goals.
  • Raise concerns about supports, staff, or quality with One Life Foundation.
  • Comply with property rules set by the MTA/SDA property provider.

11 Section 73G of the NDIS Act

Is this participant subject to Section 73G?Yes     /     No

Note: Section 73G of the NDIS Act refers to a participant who is provided with personal support in their own home and may experience a level of risk if the same individual NDIS worker provides that support for an extended period. This risk is likely to be greater where the participant lives alone.

For participants subject to this requirement, One Life Foundation will:

  • Document the assessment of the participant's risk factor using the Initial Assessment and Support Plan, Participant Disaster and Risk Assessment, and Participant Home Risk Assessment.
  • Provide a copy of all assessments to the participant (unless they opt out) and retain a copy on file.
  • Review the assessment annually or when the participant's circumstances change.
  • Register the participant on the High-Risk Participant Register and delegate specific support workers accordingly.
  • Ensure the Operations Manager reviews service implementation every 3 months by someone other than the support workers.
  • Conduct face-to-face welfare checks at the participant's home (without the support worker present) to verify performance, safety, and wellbeing.

12 Emergency Preparedness

One Life Foundation will develop an emergency response plan to respond to any unplanned event that may cause death, significant injury, disruption to operations, or physical/environmental damage.

The Operations Manager or Supervisor will ensure that:

  • An Emergency Evacuation diagram is available for each site.
  • An Individual Disaster Management Plan is created for each participant using the Participant Disaster and Risk Assessment.
  • All support workers are trained on emergency response procedures.
  • Support workers receive a copy of the participant's individual disaster management plan so they are fully aware of the participant's health condition and required action plans.

13 Cancellation and Short Notice Policy

In accordance with the NDIS Pricing Arrangements and Price Limits:

  • Cancellations with 2+ clear business days' notice — no cancellation fee.
  • Short notice (less than 2 clear business days) or no-show — up to 100% of the agreed fee may be charged.
  • One Life Foundation will attempt to reallocate the worker before charging a cancellation fee.
  • No cancellation fee where the cancellation is due to circumstances beyond the participant's control (e.g. hospitalisation).
  • If One Life Foundation cancels a support (e.g. staff illness), a replacement will be arranged where possible and no charge will apply for undelivered supports.

The participant confirms these cancellation terms were explained before signing.

14 Incident Management

One Life Foundation manages incidents in accordance with the NDIS (Incident Management and Reportable Incidents) Rules 2018:

  • All incidents are reported, recorded, investigated, and reviewed.
  • The participant will be notified of any incident that affects them.
  • Reportable incidents (death, serious injury, abuse/neglect, sexual misconduct, unauthorised restrictive practices) are reported to the NDIS Commission within 24 hours.

Restrictive Practices

One Life Foundation does not use unauthorised restrictive practices. Where a Behaviour Support Plan includes regulated restrictive practices, these must be authorised through the relevant state process, documented in a current BSP by a registered practitioner, implemented only by trained staff, and reviewed regularly with the goal of reduction and elimination.

15 Complaints, Feedback, and Disputes

Making a complaint will not affect your supports or this agreement. To raise a concern:

  • Step 1: Raise the matter with your support worker or team leader.
  • Step 2: If unresolved, formal complaint to (04) 5222 7799 or support@olfndisservices.com.au.
  • Step 3: Acknowledged within 2 business days, resolved within 14 business days.
  • Step 4: If unsatisfied, escalate to the NDIS Commission (1800 035 544) or NSW Ombudsman (1800 451 524).

The participant may involve an independent advocate at any time via disabilityadvocacyfinder.dss.gov.au or 1800 800 110.

Dispute Resolution

  • Direct negotiation within 7 business days.
  • Mediation by an independent mediator if unresolved.
  • Referral to the NDIS Commission if still unresolved.

Supports continue during disputes unless there is an immediate safety risk.

17 Termination and Transition

Participant-Initiated

  • End this agreement at any time with no more than 14 days' notice. No exit fees.
  • Right to change support provider at any time without penalty.

Provider-Initiated

One Life Foundation may terminate with minimum 14 days' notice if:

  • Serious or repeated breach of participant responsibilities that cannot be resolved.
  • Immediate and ongoing safety risk to workers, residents, or the participant.
  • NDIS funding is exhausted, withdrawn, or not renewed.
  • The participant transitions to longer-term accommodation or vacates the MTA property.
  • The MTA period (90 days) expires and no extension has been approved by the NDIA.

Continuity of Supports

  • Supports continue during the notice period.
  • With consent, a comprehensive handover is provided to the incoming provider.
  • Claims cease after the termination date.
  • Records are retained securely per legal requirements.

One Life Foundation will not cease supports abruptly without ensuring the participant's safety.
