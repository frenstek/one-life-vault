3. Medications Covered by This Consent

This consent applies to all medications listed on the participant's current Medication Purpose Form and/or Webster pack, including any updates made by the prescribing doctor.

Note: A current Medication Purpose Form must be completed and kept on file alongside this consent. Any changes to medication must be updated on both forms and communicated to all support workers.

4. Participant / Guardian Acknowledgement

By signing this form, I acknowledge and agree that:

  1. I have provided accurate information about the participant's medications, allergies, and medical conditions.
  2. Support workers will only administer medications from a Webster/Blister pack or as specifically directed by a registered health professional.
  3. Support workers are not qualified medical professionals and will not make clinical decisions regarding medication.
  4. All medication administration will be recorded in the participant's progress notes and medication chart.
  5. I will notify One Life Foundation immediately of any changes to the participant's medication, dosage, or medical condition.
  6. I understand I may withdraw this consent at any time by providing written notice to One Life Foundation.
  7. In the event of a medication error or adverse reaction, support workers will follow emergency procedures and notify the participant's GP and guardian/NOK immediately.
