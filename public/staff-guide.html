01 Logging In

The Vault is a web application — access it through your phone or computer browser. No app download required.

1

Open your browser

On your phone, tablet, or computer, open Chrome, Safari, or any web browser.

2

Go to the website

Type in the address bar: onelifefoundationndis.app and press Enter.

3

Enter your email

Type the email address your manager gave you (e.g. yourname@onelifefoundation.com.au).

4

Enter your password

Type the password you were given. If you've forgotten it, contact your manager — they can reset it.

5

Click "Sign In"

You'll be taken to the Dashboard — the home page of the system.

03 Writing Progress Notes

Progress notes are the most important thing you'll do in the Vault. Every shift, write a note for each participant you supported. This is an NDIS requirement.

1

Go to Progress Notes

Click "Progress Notes" in the sidebar under the Participants section.

2

Click "Add Note"

Click the blue "Add Note" button in the top-right corner. A form will pop up.

3

Fill in Shift Details

Select the participant, enter your name, choose the date, and pick the shift type.

4

Write the Progress Note

In the main text box, describe what happened — behaviour, activities, mood, anything notable. Be factual and professional.

5

Health & Wellbeing (optional)

Select mood, sleep quality, add BSL readings, and any medication notes if relevant.

6

Daily Activities (optional)

Record meals (breakfast, lunch, dinner, snacks) and activities throughout the shift.

7

Note any Concerns

If there are concerns, write them in the Concerns field. These get highlighted for managers.

8

Click "Save Note"

Review and click Save. Your note appears in the list and on the participant's profile.

💡
Tips for Good NotesWrite factually — what you observed, not opinions. Use the participant's name. Include times. E.g. "John was calm throughout the morning. Had breakfast at 7:30am (cereal and toast). Went for a walk at 10am for 30 minutes."
⚠️
Don't Forget ConcernsIf something worrying happened — unusual behaviour, refusal of medication, injury, mood change — always log it in the Concerns field. This alerts managers immediately.

04 Filing an Incident Report

Incidents must be reported as soon as possible. This includes injuries, falls, medication errors, behaviour of concern, aggression, property damage, or anything unusual. This is an NDIS requirement.

1

Go to Incidents

Click "Incidents" in the sidebar under the Participants section.

2

Click "Report Incident"

Click the "Report Incident" button. The incident form will open.

3

Enter the incident title

Brief, clear title. E.g. "Participant fall in bathroom" or "Medication refusal".

4

Select severity and category

Choose from the dropdowns (see reference below).

5

Fill in date, time, and location

When and where? Be specific — "Bathroom at 123 Smith St" not just "house".

6

Link the participant

Select the participant involved. Also link the property if relevant.

7

Write a detailed description

Explain exactly what happened — who, what, when, sequence of events. Be factual.

8

Record immediate actions taken

E.g. "Applied first aid", "Called ambulance", "Notified manager".

9

Enter your name and submit

Type your name in "Reported By" and click "Submit".

Severity Levels
LevelWhen to UseExamples
LowMinor, no injuryNear-miss, minor property damage, verbal disagreement
MediumSome impact, minor injury possibleMinor fall (no injury), medication given late
HighSignificant, injury or riskFall with injury, medication error, aggression
CriticalSerious harm, emergency servicesHospitalisation, abuse/neglect, missing person
Incident Categories
Injury Medication Error Behaviour of Concern Property Damage Abuse / Neglect Missing Person Fall Aggression Other
⚠️
Report ImmediatelyDo not wait until end of shift. File the report as soon as the situation is safe. For Critical incidents, also call your manager immediately.
📄
PDF DownloadAfter filing, you can download a PDF copy of any incident by clicking the download icon. Useful for NDIS reporting.

05 Viewing Participant Profiles

Before every shift, check the participant's profile to review care needs, medications, allergies, and recent notes.

1

Go to Participants

Click "Participants" in the sidebar. You'll see a list of all participants.

2

Find the participant

Search by name or NDIS number, or scroll through the list.

3

Click "View Profile"

Click their name or "View Profile" to open the full profile.

⚡ Important Alerts (Read First!)

At the top of every profile, you'll see highlighted alerts for Allergies, Medications, Dietary requirements, Mobility, and Communication needs. Always read these before starting your shift.

Profile Tabs
TabWhat's Inside
📝 Progress NotesAll shift notes for this participant. You can add notes from here too.
⚠️ IncidentsAll incident reports linked to this participant.
📎 FilesCare plans, BSPs, service agreements, medical files.
💊 MedicationsFull medication list — dosage, frequency, prescriber, instructions.
📈 MonitoringHealth monitoring and tracking data.
👤 ContactsEmergency contacts, GP, specialists, guardians, family.
📖 Support PlanGoals, daily needs, behaviour support, risk management, overnight support.
💡
Before Every ShiftCheck the participant's profile before you start. Look at alerts, recent progress notes, and the medications tab. This helps you provide the best care and stay informed of changes.

06 Clocking In & Out
1

Go to Time Clock

Click "Time Clock" in the sidebar under the Staff section.

2

Clock In

Click the "Clock In" button when you arrive and are ready to start work.

3

Clock Out

At the end of your shift, come back and click "Clock Out".

⚠️
Don't ForgetAlways clock out. If you forget, contact your manager. Accurate time records are important for payroll and NDIS billing.
07 Checking the Shift Roster
1

Go to Shift Roster

Click "Shift Roster" in the sidebar under the Staff section.

2

View your shifts

See a calendar view of shifts. Check date, time, location, and which participant you're supporting.

📅
Check RegularlyCheck your roster at the start of each week. Shifts can change, so stay up to date.
08 Documents & Policies

The Vault stores all company policies, procedures, and forms. Search, browse by department, and view directly in your browser.

1

Click "All Documents" or browse by department

Use "All Documents" for everything, or expand a department (Clinical, Operations, HR) to filter.

2

Search for what you need

Use the search bar. E.g. type "medication" to find Medication Management Policy.

3

Click to view

Click any document to view in the browser. PDFs and Word docs open in-system.

💡
Key Documents to KnowFamiliarise yourself with: Medication Management Policy, Incident Reporting Procedure, Behaviour Support Guidelines, WHS Policy, and each participant's Care Plan (profile → Files tab).

10 Quick Reference Cheat Sheet
Every Shift Checklist
  • Log in to onelifefoundationndis.app
  • Clock in on the Time Clock page
  • Check participant's profile — read Alerts, Medications, and recent Notes
  • Check Announcements for any company updates
  • During shift: note observations, meals, activities, and concerns
  • If an incident occurs: file an Incident Report immediately
  • End of shift: write a Progress Note for each participant supported
  • Clock out on the Time Clock page
